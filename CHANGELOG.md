# Changelog

## 0.7.0
- **New themes:** slate (blue-grey) and paper (warm beige), in the style and as skin files.
- **Skin for every screen:** it can now be used on its own at any width, not only on phones.
- **New setting:** "Collapse long tag lists (work page)", off by default. Long tag lists show about three lines with a "more" hint and open on hover (style only; needs a recent Chrome).
- **Buttons:** hover, focus and pressed states are now flat, with a colour change instead of AO3's 3D bevel.
- **Skin:** notices have proper padding and no inner shadow; on profiles the avatar sits small beside the name.
- **Account pages:** the Pseuds list in the sidebar is a flat card.
- Bug fixes and tweaks.
