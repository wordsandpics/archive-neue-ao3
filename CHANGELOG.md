# Changelog

## 0.7.3
Skin fixes (the userstyle is unchanged).
- **Filters:** on work lists the filter panel is beside the list again, not at the bottom of the page.
- **Tablets:** the page fills the screen; the story and its comments keep a comfortable width.
- **Skin edit page:** the Media checkboxes show again, and "Add parent skin" is no longer cut off.

## 0.7.2
- **Tags in work lists:** warnings, relationships and characters, and additional tags now look different from each other, with a little space between the groups (userstyle and skins).
- **Work info on the skins:** easier to scan, with smaller labels and a thin line above the stats.
- **Visited links:** hovering one keeps the theme's link colour (it turned near-black on dark themes).
- **Guest comments:** a small round avatar, like everyone else's.
- **Chapter index:** "Go" looks like a button.
- **Skin menus:** the header menus stay open when you move the mouse into them, are no longer covered by page links, and fit their text.
- **Skin fixes:** the Skins page picture no longer covers titles, the comment box has no extra line, and a chapter's summary sits a little below its title.

## 0.7.1
- **Stats page:** the sort buttons are clickable again, and "Sort By" lines up with them.
- **Search box:** the text keeps the theme colour while you type (it turned dark grey on dark themes).
- **Restricted works:** the padlock stays before the title, also on two-line titles.
- **Collections:** a collection's home page shows its name once, with a small avatar and matching buttons (userstyle and skins).

## 0.7.0
- **New themes:** slate (blue-grey) and paper (warm beige), in the style and as skin files.
- **Responsive skin:** it can now be used on its own at any width, not only on phones.
- **New setting:** "Collapse long tag lists (work page)", off by default. Long tag lists show about three lines with a "more" hint and open on hover (style only; needs a recent Chrome).
- **Bug fixes and tweaks:** Button states, submenus, notices and other small changes. 

## 0.6.x
First public releases.
